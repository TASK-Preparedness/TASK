## Shared ownership design
Reuse `task_comms.ownership.DeviceClaim` for a future canonical `bt:<UPPERCASE-MAC>` key, held for the entire connected lifecycle; never two claims for differently formatted addresses. Acquire, check evidence and transport state, then open only the authorized target; release in finally. Unknown/hidden ownership, changed identity, missing checks or lock error means refuse, not free. `holders()` cannot enumerate every process or Bluetooth SPP client (`/usr/lib/python3/dist-packages/task_comms/ownership.py:3-6,18-48`).

Core logical assignments do not replace OS leases. Mesh's resolved-tty locks under `task/mesh-devices` differ from comms' sanitized-key `task/locks` (`/usr/lib/task-mesh/task_mesh/hub/locks.py:106-117`; `/usr/lib/python3/dist-packages/task_comms/ownership.py:54-74`). Radio's hard deny-list is invariant, not a fallback try-lock. Do not unify namespaces or service orchestration during Radio r3; that would require a separately approved shared ownership migration. Firmware Manager remains a separate project; this report does not authorize releasing/flashing the Heltec.

Device identity is revalidated immediately before any future physical open; aliases/symlinks, canonical path, major/minor and USB identity checked together. Denied devices stay denied after tty renumbering. No /dev/ttyUSB* enumeration followed by speculative opens. Only simulator identities accepted in B; real paths/MACs rejected.
